## 1. α-λ accuracy (α = 20%): share of the 100 engines whose predicted RUL is within ±20% of the true RUL at fraction λ of their life

| model | λ=0.25 | λ=0.5 | λ=0.75 | λ=0.9 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B0 | 56% | 42% | 34% | 13% |
| B1 | 20% | 63% | 4% | 7% |
| M1 | 52% | 51% | 71% | 60% |

Early in life (λ = 0.25, true RUL ≈ 150) a model capped at R_max can't be within ±20 % by construction: the α-λ criterion only becomes meaningful once degradation is observable. B1's 63 % at λ = 0.5 is an artefact: its fallback value R_max = 100 happens to be close to the true RUL at mid-life; at λ = 0.75-0.9, when its trend kicks in, it lags (late bias).

## 2. Decision rule at L = 20 cycles

Alert at the first cycle where the bound <= L; maintenance done L cycles later. *Failure* = the engine fails before the maintenance (or no alert at all). *Wasted* = useful life left at the maintenance, averaged over **successful** interventions only (100 − failures engines), so it is unreliable when failures are frequent.

| model | decision bound | failures /100 | no alert | mean wasted cycles | median RUL at alert |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B0 | q05 | 5 | 0 | 61.8 | 68.0 |
| B0 | q50 | 76 | 69 | 36.9 | 38.0 |
| B1 | q05 | 0 | 0 | 49.8 | 42.0 |
| B1 | q50 | 94 | 0 | 78.5 | 12.0 |
| M1 | q05 | 0 | 0 | 14.3 | 33.0 |
| M1 | q50 | 48 | 0 | 4.1 | 20.0 |
