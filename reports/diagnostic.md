- Causal Hampel filter (3 robust sigmas, median/MAD of the 21 previous cycles, applied to the causally detrended signal). **Reference: the same filter flags 2.2% of pure Gaussian white noise** (short windows make MAD noisy). Sensor flag rates match this reference: no evidence of gross errors, spikes or saturation. Points would be **flagged, not removed** (a spike can be an early degradation sign).
- s17 is coarsely quantised (integer steps, a handful of distinct values): its MAD is often 0 or 1 step, so its Hampel rate is inflated by quantisation, not by outliers. Quantisation also limits how early it can reveal degradation.
- Drift-over-life is only a few times the cycle-to-cycle noise: a single cycle is not informative, **trailing smoothing / regression over a window** is needed. Smoothing must be causal (trailing mean, EWM, Kalman) - never centred windows, filtfilt or Savitzky-Golay.

## 6. Consequences for the next phases

1. Drop the constant channels; work with the trending sensors (re-selected per fold).
2. No imputation / deduplication needed (clean grid) - the preparation log will still record row counts at each step.

4. Validation units truncated at random cut points to reproduce test censoring.
5. Early life shows no visible trend (see figures): motivates a capped RUL target for the direct regression (P-F curve argument).
